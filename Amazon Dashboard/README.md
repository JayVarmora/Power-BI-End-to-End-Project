# 📊 Amazon E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
📌 Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive E-commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI.

It analyzes business performance across sales, orders, profit, customers, products, and regions using interactive visuals, KPIs, and trend analysis to support data-driven decision making.


## 🧩 Dashboard 1: Executive Sales Overview

Purpose: High-level business performance tracking.

Key Insights:

•	Total Revenue: $24.9M

•	Total Orders: 25.2K

•	Total Profit: $10.5M

•	Return Rate: 2.1%

•	Monthly revenue shows a consistent upward trend with forecasted growth.

•	Accessories lead in order volume, followed by Bikes and Clothing.

•	Top products like Water Bottle (30 oz) and Sport-100 Helmets contribute significantly to profit.

Business Value:
Helps leadership quickly assess overall growth, profitability, and category performance.


## 🌍 Dashboard 2: Regional & Geographic Analysis

Purpose: Understand sales distribution across regions.

Key Insights:

•	Orders visualized by country using a map-based view.

•	Strong performance in North America, followed by Europe and Pacific regions.

•	Region tabs enable easy comparison between continents.

Business Value:
Identifies high-performing regions and supports regional expansion and marketing strategy.


## 🛒 Dashboard 3: Product Performance & Target Analysis

Purpose: Product-level deep dive and target tracking.

Key Insights:

•	Selected product analysis (e.g., Water Bottle – 30 oz)

•	Monthly Orders, Revenue, and Profit compared against targets using gauge visuals.

•	Revenue growth of 275%+ over the selected time period.

•	Interactive metric selection (Orders, Revenue, Profit, Returns).

Business Value:
Supports product optimization, pricing decisions, and performance monitoring against targets.


## 👥 Dashboard 4: Customer & Demographic Analysis

Purpose: Analyze customer behavior and segmentation.

Key Insights:

•	Total Customers: 17.4K

•	Revenue per Customer: $1,431

•	Customer growth trend shows strong increase over time.

•	Orders segmented by income level and occupation.

•	Top customers identified by revenue contribution.

•	High contribution from professional and skilled manual segments.

Business Value:
Enables customer segmentation, loyalty analysis, and targeted marketing strategies.


## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used

•	Power BI

•	DAX Measures & Calculated Columns

•	Date Hierarchies & Time Intelligence

•	KPI Cards, Line Charts, Bar Charts, Maps, Gauges

•	Interactive Slicers & Drill-Down Analysis
