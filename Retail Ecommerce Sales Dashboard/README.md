# 📊 Retail E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive E-Commerce Sales Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and payment trends.
The dashboard enables quick business insights through KPIs, category analysis, monthly trends, and regional performance.

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

•	Total Sales Amount: 438K

•	Total Profit: 37K

•	Total Orders: 5,615

•	Average Order Value: 121K

These KPIs provide a high-level snapshot of overall business performance.

## 📈 Sales & Profit Analysis

•	Monthly Profit Trend highlights fluctuations across months, identifying both profit peaks and loss periods.

•	Strong profit growth observed during February, March, and November.

•	Negative profit months help identify operational or cost-related issues requiring attention.

## 🛍️ Category & Sub-Category Insights

•	Order Quantity by Category:

- Clothing: 63% (highest contribution)

- Electronics: 21%

- Furniture: 17%

•	Profit by Sub-Category:

- Top contributors: Printers, Bookcases, Sarees

- Lower-profit areas: Tables and Accessories

- This analysis helps identify high-performing products and areas for improvement.

## 💳 Payment Mode Analysis

•	Cash on Delivery (COD): 44% – most preferred payment method

•	UPI: 21%

•	Debit Card: 13%

•	Credit Card: 12%

•	EMI: 10%

These insights support payment strategy optimization and customer convenience planning.

## 👥 Customer Analysis

•	Top customers identified based on total purchase amount.

•	Revenue distribution by customer highlights key revenue-driving buyers.

•	Enables focused customer retention and loyalty strategies.

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

•	Quarter-wise slicers (Q1–Q4)

•	Category and sub-category filtering

•	Dynamic visuals with drill-down capability

•	Clean and modern UI with dark theme for better readability

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

•	Power BI

•	DAX Measures

•	Data Modeling & Relationships

•	KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Donut Charts, Line Charts

•	Interactive Slicers

## ✅ Conclusion

This dashboard provides a complete view of sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and payment trends.
It demonstrates strong skills in data visualization, business analysis, and storytelling using Power BI, making it suitable for real-world business analytics and decision-making.
