# 🏥 US Healthcare Industry Analytics Dashboard (2020) – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes **US Healthcare Industry data for the year 2020**, focusing on **payments, patients, hospitals, providers, and CPT utilization**.  
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand **financial performance, patient demographics, regional trends, and provider efficiency** through interactive and data-driven insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track **Gross Expense, Insurance Pay, Patient Pay, and CPT Units**
- Analyze **patient demographics and behavior**
- Evaluate **hospital performance and bad debts**
- Understand **provider distribution, FTE, and specialty efficiency**
- Identify **regional and monthly healthcare trends**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Insights

### 1️⃣ Executive Summary
**Purpose:** High-level financial and operational overview  
**Key KPIs:**
- Gross Expense: **8.42M**
- Insurance Pay: **5M**
- Patient Pay: **1.02M**
- CPT Units: **239K**
- Total Patients: **5,117**

**Insights:**
- Significant drop in payments compared to previous months
- CPT units peak in early months and decline mid-year
- Medicare is the largest CPT contributor

---

### 2️⃣ Patient Analysis
**Purpose:** Understand patient demographics and lifestyle patterns  
**Key Dimensions:**
- Gender, Ethnicity, Blood Group
- Tobacco, Alcohol, Diet, Exercise habits
- Monthly Patient Pay trend

**Insights:**
- Majority patients are **White ethnicity**
- **O+ and A+** are the most common blood groups
- Patient payments spike towards **December**
- Lifestyle factors show strong variation across patients

---

### 3️⃣ Geographic Analysis (Patient by State)
**Purpose:** Regional distribution of patients across the US  
**Features:**
- Interactive US map
- Region filters: Midwest, Northeast, South, West

**Insights:**
- Higher patient concentration in **South and East regions**
- Urban states contribute significantly to patient volume

---

### 4️⃣ Hospital Analysis
**Purpose:** Evaluate hospital financial health and performance  
**Key Metrics:**
- ARGE Ratio & IPTP Ratio by month
- Total AR: **3.97M**
- Hospital Count: **11**
- Total CPT Grouping: **11**

**Insights:**
- Radiology and Surgery generate the highest expenses
- Certain hospitals show higher **bad debts**
- Performance varies significantly by region

---

### 5️⃣ Provider Analysis
**Purpose:** Analyze healthcare providers and specialties  
**Key Metrics:**
- Total Providers: **932**
- Provider FTE: **711.74**
- Specialties: **34**
- Avg FTE per Provider: **0.76**

**Insights:**
- South region has the highest physician count
- Internal Medicine leads in CPT units
- Provider workload varies strongly by specialty
